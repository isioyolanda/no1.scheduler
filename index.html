My week, ticked off

Welcome!

So I’ve been enjoying productive days and thought this would be a fun way to get you to enjoy them too!

Here’s how to get started:

  1. Tap any date on the calendar to open that day, then tick things off as you do them.
  2. Tap “Edit schedule” to make it yours. Type into any hour, save, then choose whether it applies to just that day or more days.
  3. Finish everything in a day to build your streak.
  4. Tap the “This week” card to see how your week went, which habits slipped, and how it compares to last week.
  5. Open “Appearance” to pick your colors or switch to dark mode.

Everything is saved in this browser on this device, so your planner stays private to you.

I know it’s currently very rudimentary but I promise it gets the job done. I’ll be rolling out new updates periodically so stay tuned and let me know if you’ve got particular features you’d like me to add. I’d actually love the feedback!
